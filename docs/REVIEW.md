# REVIEW

Review roles:
- SEO Lead: finds search and structure weaknesses.
- Content Editor: removes filler and improves clarity.
- Performance Marketing Expert: adds practical depth.
- Google Helpful Content Reviewer: tries to reject the article.
- Business Owner: checks whether the material builds trust.
- Chief Editor: rewrites weak parts.

Minimum acceptance:
- useful beyond competitors;
- no filler;
- clear next steps;
- no unsupported claims;
- no confidential methods;
- natural internal links;
- human rhythm.
